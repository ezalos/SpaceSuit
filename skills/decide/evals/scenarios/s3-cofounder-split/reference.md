Lens: negotiation + design. Principal: Louis, with agreement needed from A and B.

Axes (obvious): who is CEO; who leads engineering.
Axes (often-missed):
1. what A wants (stated or asked, not assumed as fact)
2. what B wants
3. investor optics (one clear CEO)
4. ownership by deliverable, no overlap
5. reversibility (a trial period or a revisit date)
6. the relationships between the three
7. Louis's research time
8. decision rights / who breaks ties
9. title inflation risk with a 4-person company

Option families (at least 5 expected):
A. classic functional split (CEO / CTO / head of ML)
B. ownership by deliverable or artifact rather than title
C. time-boxed trial with a revisit date
D. newcomer as VP or lead engineering without a co-founder title
E. research co-owned with explicit boundaries
F. defer titles, assign deliverables first
G. bring a menu to the dinner and design it together

MUSTs: no split imposed without A and B; no overlapping ownership; nothing that locks
titles permanently before a trial.
Late constraint (given after the first reply): research must be owned by one person,
and A wants research too. The final output must absorb it without starting over.
Expected shape: a menu of splits, each with what each person gets and when it breaks,
the invariants every split keeps, and the questions to ask A and B. No single split
presented as the answer.
