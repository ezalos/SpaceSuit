Lens: choice, quick mode. Principal: Louis.

Axes (obvious): monthly cost.
Axes (often-missed):
1. flexibility / option value (the possible move)
2. probability and timing of moving
3. break fee
4. renewal risk on the 1-year lease

Option families (at least 3 expected in quick mode):
A. 3-year lease
B. 1-year lease
C. negotiate a break clause into the 3-year lease
D. a 2-year lease or other middle term
E. 1-year lease with a capped renewal

MUSTs: do not lock into an unbreakable long commitment while a move is likely.
Expected shape: quick (about one screen); the irreversibility of the 3-year lease is
flagged; at least one break-even style comparison; at most one question.
