Lens: negotiation. Principal: Louis. Counterparty: the startup (interests unstated).

Axes (obvious): base salary; equity amount.
Axes (often-missed):
1. leverage timing (the other process ends after the deadline: extension or sequencing)
2. side-work / IP assignment clause (keeping the consulting gig)
3. start date (a month off)
4. location / remote terms
5. research or learning time
6. relationship with the hiring manager
7. equity terms (strike, acceleration, exercise window, refresh)
8. title or scope
9. downside protection (severance, signing bonus)

Option families (at least 5 expected):
A. accept as offered
B. counter on cash
C. counter on equity or its terms
D. several equivalent packages offered at once (MESO-style)
E. ask for a deadline extension / align with the other process
F. non-cash asks (start date, remote, side-gig clause, title, research time)
G. decline and stay (BATNA)

MUSTs: do not let the offer lapse by accident; do not sign an IP clause that kills
the side gig; do not bluff about the other process.
Expected shape: a menu of packages with what each side gets, assumptions about the
startup's interests labelled, and an opening question or ask. No single "the answer".
